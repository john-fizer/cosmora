# Skill: Career & Lot of Spirit

## Purpose
Analyze the native's career potential, vocation, and professional life through the 10th house, Midheaven, Lot of Spirit, and relevant planets.

## Inputs Required
- 10th house sign and planets within it
- Midheaven degree and sign
- Lord of the 10th house (its sign, house, dignity, condition)
- Lot of Spirit (sign, house, dignity of its lord)
- Lord of Lot of Spirit
- Saturn's condition (career structures and responsibilities)
- Any planets aspecting the MC

## The Three Career Pillars
1. **10th House & MC** — public reputation, role in the world, career path
2. **Lot of Spirit** — what activates the native's will and vocation (more personal than 10th)
3. **Lord of Spirit's Releasing** — the timing system that reveals career chapters

## Interpretation Rules

### 10th House Analysis
- Planets in the 10th are highly visible and public — they color the career
- The sign on the 10th shows the style of career expression
- The lord of the 10th, by its condition and house placement, shows how career is supported or challenged

### Lot of Spirit Analysis
- Lot of Spirit sign reveals the domain of vocation and soul purpose
- Its lord's condition reveals how accessible career fulfillment is
- When the Lot of Spirit is in an angular sign: vocation is a primary life focus
- When the lord of Spirit is debilitated: the native's sense of purpose may take longer to develop

### Saturn and Career
- Saturn in the 10th or aspecting the MC: career requires sustained effort, but can bring lasting authority
- Saturn as lord of the 10th: a serious, structured career path; timing often comes later in life

### Timing
- Annual profection to the 10th house → a career-activated year
- Zodiacal releasing from Spirit into peak/angular signs → major career advance
- Transits by Jupiter or Saturn to the MC → public shifts

## Interpretation Pattern
"Your career is signified by [10th house sign] on the MC, with [planet if any] in the 10th. The lord of your career house is [planet], placed in [sign/house] — indicating [interpretation]. Your Lot of Spirit falls in [sign/house], with [lord] as its ruler. This suggests your vocation is oriented around [theme]. [Timing context if active]."

## Guardrails
- Career does not mean employment — it means the native's role in the world and contribution.
- Do not predict specific jobs or salaries.
- A difficult 10th house or Spirit lord signals effort required, not permanent failure.
- Women's charts historically were read the same way — do not apply gendered assumptions.
