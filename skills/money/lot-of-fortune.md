# Skill: Money, Resources & Lot of Fortune

## Purpose
Analyze the native's material resources, financial patterns, and bodily wellbeing through the 2nd house, 8th house, Lot of Fortune, and relevant planets.

## Inputs Required
- 2nd house sign and planets
- Lord of the 2nd (sign, house, dignity, condition)
- 8th house sign and planets (shared resources, inheritance, debt)
- Lot of Fortune (sign, house)
- Lord of Fortune
- Jupiter's condition (abundance)
- Venus's condition (money as pleasure/value)
- Current profection — is the 2nd or 8th house activated?

## Interpretation Areas

### 2nd House — Personal Resources
- The sign on the 2nd describes how the native earns and values money
- The lord of the 2nd, by condition, shows whether financial resources are readily available or require effort
- Planets in the 2nd color financial life directly

### Lot of Fortune — Material & Bodily Circumstances
- Fortune's sign and house indicate the domain where material life is most active
- Its lord's condition shows the quality of material circumstances overall
- Zodiacal releasing from Fortune charts the major chapters of physical and material life

### 8th House — Shared Resources
- Inheritance, loans, partner's money, taxes, shared assets
- Malefics in the 8th may indicate debt or complex shared finances
- Benefics may indicate inheritance or fortunate shared resources

### Jupiter and Venus — Abundance Indicators
- A well-placed Jupiter supports material expansion and financial opportunity
- Venus in good condition supports the enjoyment of resources and pleasurable income

## Interpretation Pattern
"Your 2nd house falls in [sign], suggesting financial themes of [quality]. The lord of your resources is [planet], in [sign/house and condition] — indicating [interpretation]. Your Lot of Fortune in [sign/house] shows material life is most active in [domain]. [If timing is relevant:] This year's profection [activates/does not activate] the financial axis."

## Guardrails
- Do not predict specific income amounts or financial outcomes.
- Financial astrology shows patterns and tendencies, not guarantees.
- Difficult 2nd house indicators mean financial life requires more work — not permanent poverty.
- Frame 8th house themes as transformation and shared resources, not only death and debt.
