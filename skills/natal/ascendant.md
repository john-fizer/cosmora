# Skill: Ascendant & Chart Ruler

## Purpose
Interpret the Ascendant (rising sign), its degree, and the planet that rules it — the chart ruler — as the primary lens of the native's life orientation, physical appearance, and approach to the world.

## Inputs Required
- Ascendant sign and degree
- Ruling planet (traditional rulership)
- Chart ruler's sign, house, dignity, and condition
- Sect (day/night)
- Aspects to the Ascendant and chart ruler

## Rules
1. The Ascendant sign describes how the native projects into the world and how they are perceived.
2. The chart ruler is the most important planet in the chart — its condition colors everything.
3. If the chart ruler is in good condition (domicile, exaltation, angular, well-aspected), the native generally has resources to work with.
4. If debilitated (detriment, fall, cadent, afflicted), life themes require more effort and development.
5. The house the chart ruler occupies shows the primary life domain the native is oriented toward.
6. Aspects to the Ascendant from benefics strengthen the native's prospects; from malefics, they add complexity or pressure.

## Interpretation Pattern
1. Name the rising sign and its essential quality.
2. Identify the chart ruler and its condition (dignity, house, aspects).
3. State what house the chart ruler occupies — this is a major life theme.
4. Note any planets conjunct the Ascendant within 8°.
5. Describe the overall life orientation suggested by these factors.

## Example Output Structure
"[Sign] rises, giving the native [qualities]. The chart is ruled by [planet], placed in [sign] in the [Nth] house — indicating [theme]. [Planet] is [dignity condition], which means [interpretation]. This native tends to approach life through the lens of [domain]."

## Guardrails
- Do not conflate the Ascendant with personality alone — it is orientation and life approach, not inner nature (that is the Moon).
- Do not make appearance predictions without acknowledging individual variation.
- Frame debilitated chart rulers as areas of growth and effort, not curses.
