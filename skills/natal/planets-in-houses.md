# Skill: Planets in Houses

## Purpose
Interpret each planet's house placement as the primary life domain where that planet's themes manifest and are most active.

## Inputs Required
- Planet name, sign, dignity
- House number (1–12)
- House rulership (what sign rules this house for this native)
- Angularity (angular = 1,4,7,10; succedent = 2,5,8,11; cadent = 3,6,9,12)

## House Meanings (Traditional)
| House | Topics |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Self, body, identity, life orientation, appearance |
| 2 | Money, possessions, livelihood, values, self-worth |
| 3 | Siblings, short travel, communication, local community, learning |
| 4 | Home, family, roots, ancestors, private life, real estate |
| 5 | Children, creativity, pleasure, romance, games, speculation |
| 6 | Health, work, daily routine, service, illness, employees |
| 7 | Marriage, partnerships, open enemies, contracts, other people |
| 8 | Death, transformation, shared resources, inheritance, debt, taboo |
| 9 | Philosophy, religion, long travel, higher education, publishing, law |
| 10 | Career, public reputation, authority, achievement, the father |
| 11 | Friends, networks, alliances, gains, hopes, groups |
| 12 | Hidden enemies, isolation, retreat, undoing, institutions, spirituality |

## Angularity Rules
- **Angular (1,4,7,10):** Planet is strong, active, and visible in these domains. Effects are more immediate and pronounced.
- **Succedent (2,5,8,11):** Planet is stable and accumulating. Effects unfold over time.
- **Cadent (3,6,9,12):** Planet is weaker and less able to act directly. Themes require more work to access.

## Interpretation Pattern
1. Name the planet and its natural significations.
2. State the house and its topic.
3. Note the angularity — how active is this planet?
4. Integrate dignity: a dignified planet in a good house is a clear asset; a debilitated planet in a difficult house is a complex area.
5. Note the house ruler and whether it receives or aspects the placed planet.

## Guardrails
- A planet in the 8th or 12th house is not automatically negative — these are complex domains.
- Angularity increases visibility and impact; it does not guarantee ease.
- Always connect house placement to the planet's broader condition.
