# Skill: Relationships & 7th House

## Purpose
Analyze relationship patterns, partnership potential, and the quality of significant relationships through the 7th house, its lord, and Venus (or Mars for attraction).

## Inputs Required
- 7th house sign
- Lord of the 7th (sign, house, dignity, condition)
- Venus's condition (sign, house, dignity, aspects)
- Mars's condition (desire, passion, conflict)
- Any planets in the 7th house
- Lot of Eros (sign and house)
- Aspects between Venus and Mars

## Interpretation Areas

### 7th House — Committed Partnership
- The sign on the 7th describes the type of partner the native attracts or seeks
- The lord of the 7th describes the quality and availability of partnership
- Planets in the 7th become significant partners or partnership themes

### Venus — Love, Attraction, Aesthetics
- Venus's sign: what the native finds beautiful, pleasurable, and desirable
- Venus's house: where love and beauty themes manifest
- Venus's dignity: how easily the native accesses love and pleasure
- Venus dignified + angular: relationships flow more naturally
- Venus debilitated or afflicted: love is harder-won but often deeply meaningful

### Mars — Desire, Passion, Conflict
- Mars's relationship to Venus reveals the native's experience of desire
- Mars square or opposite Venus: attraction is strong but may include tension
- Mars conjunct Venus: passionate but potentially combustive in love

### Lot of Eros
- Shows where erotic desire and emotional attraction are strongest
- A more personal indicator of relationship energy than the 7th

## Interpretation Pattern
"Your 7th house falls in [sign], suggesting you attract partners who are [qualities]. The lord of this house is [planet], in [sign/house] — indicating [theme]. Venus, representing your capacity for love, is [dignity/condition] in [sign], suggesting [interpretation]. [If timing is active:] Your current profection year [activates/does not activate] the 7th house."

## Guardrails
- Do not predict specific relationship outcomes or marriage timing with certainty.
- Relationship patterns from the chart describe tendencies and themes, not destiny.
- 7th house malefics do not mean "no relationships" — they often indicate significant, complex partnerships.
- Do not use astrology to evaluate whether a specific named person is a good partner.
