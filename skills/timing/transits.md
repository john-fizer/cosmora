# Skill: Transits

## Purpose
Interpret current and upcoming transits of moving planets over natal planets, angles, and house cusps. Transits activate natal promise — they do not create new themes, they trigger what is already in the chart.

## Inputs Required
- Current planetary positions (transiting bodies)
- Natal planets, angles, and house cusps
- Active annual profection (lord of year and activated house)
- Orb settings (see below)

## Orb Settings (Default)
| Aspect | Approaching Orb | Separating Orb |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| Conjunction | 8° | 4° |
| Opposition | 8° | 4° |
| Trine | 7° | 3° |
| Square | 7° | 3° |
| Sextile | 5° | 2° |
| Quincunx | 3° | 1° |

## Transit Hierarchy (Priority Order)
1. Transits to the **Lord of the Year** — highest priority all year
2. Transits to **angles** (ASC, MC, DSC, IC)
3. Transits through the **activated profection house**
4. Transits by **outer planets** (Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) — long-lasting
5. Transits by **Jupiter** — expansion, opportunity, growth
6. Transits by **Mars** — activation, conflict, energy
7. Transits by **inner planets** (Sun, Venus, Mercury) — shorter, more frequent

## Transit Interpretation Rules
1. **The natal planet describes the domain** — a transit to natal Moon affects emotional life, home, and relationships.
2. **The transiting planet brings its nature** — Saturn transits bring structure, responsibility, or limitation; Jupiter brings expansion.
3. **The aspect type modifies the quality** — conjunctions merge energies; squares create tension; trines flow; oppositions pull apart.
4. **Retrograde passes** — a planet may transit a point 2–3 times. The first pass introduces the theme; the retrograde reviews it; the direct pass resolves it.
5. **Duration matters** — outer planet transits (Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) last months to years. Inner planet transits last days.
6. **Eclipses** — solar and lunar eclipses on natal points are among the most activating events. Flag them when they hit within 2°.

## Interpretation Pattern
"[Transiting planet] is [aspect] your natal [natal planet/angle] in [house]. This transit began [date] and completes [date]. [Transiting planet]'s nature suggests [theme]. Since your natal [planet] rules [house topics], this transit activates [domain]. With [Lord of Year] also active, this period is especially significant."

## Guardrails
- A malefic transit is not a catastrophe prediction — it is a period requiring attention.
- Do not quote exact dates as certain — orbs shift and human experience varies.
- Always connect the transit to the natal promise: a transiting Jupiter cannot give what the natal chart does not promise.
- Frame transits as weather, not fate: "This period may bring [theme] — how you navigate it depends on your choices."
