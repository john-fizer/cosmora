# Skill: Zodiacal Releasing

## Purpose
Calculate and interpret zodiacal releasing periods from the Lot of Fortune (for bodily/material life) and the Lot of Spirit (for career/soul activity). This technique reveals major life chapters and peak periods.

## Inputs Required
- Lot of Fortune longitude
- Lot of Spirit longitude
- Sect (day/night) — affects lot calculation
- Native's birth date
- Current date

## Lot Formulas (Sect-Aware)
```
Day chart:
  Lot of Fortune = ASC + Moon − Sun
  Lot of Spirit  = ASC + Sun − Moon

Night chart:
  Lot of Fortune = ASC + Sun − Moon
  Lot of Spirit  = ASC + Moon − Sun
```

## Levels
Zodiacal releasing has 4 levels. Each level runs within the period above it:
- **Level 1:** Major chapter of life (years to decades)
- **Level 2:** Sub-chapter within L1 (months to years)
- **Level 3:** Phase within L2 (weeks to months)
- **Level 4:** Granular timing (days to weeks)

## Key Events to Detect
- **Peak Periods:** When releasing reaches an angular sign from the Lot (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th)
- **Loosing of the Bond (LB):** When L1 shifts to an opposite sign or L2 hits a critical sign — often correlates with major life changes, separations, or turning points
- **Completion periods:** End of a major L1 period
- **Sign changes at any level:** Shift in themes and tone

## Interpretation Rules
1. **Releasing from Fortune:** Describes bodily health, material circumstances, and physical life events
2. **Releasing from Spirit:** Describes career, vocation, reputation, and soul purpose — the more important lot for life achievement
3. **Peak periods from Spirit in angular signs** often correlate with major career advances and public visibility
4. **When Spirit and Fortune peak simultaneously:** A major convergence — flag as highly significant
5. **Loosing of the Bond:** Alert the native — this is a significant turning point period (typically 3–12 months)

## Interpretation Pattern
"From the Lot of [Fortune/Spirit], you are currently in a [Sign] L1 period — ruled by [planet]. This period began [date] and runs until [date]. Within this, your L2 period is [Sign], which began [date]. The current themes are [interpretation based on signs and their rulers]. [If peak:] You are in a peak period — a window of greater vitality and forward movement. [If LB:] You are approaching a Loosing of the Bond around [date] — expect a significant transition in your [Fortune/Spirit] domain."

## Guardrails
- Zodiacal releasing describes life chapters — it does not guarantee specific events.
- A difficult sign in releasing (e.g., malefic-ruled) describes a more challenging chapter, not a catastrophe.
- Loosing of the Bond is significant but its expression varies enormously by chart and life circumstances.
- Always cross-reference with annual profections and transits to confirm significance.
