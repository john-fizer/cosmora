# Skill: Essential Dignity

## Purpose
Assess each planet's essential dignity — its strength or weakness based solely on sign placement. Dignity indicates the planet's ability to perform its natural function effectively.

## Inputs Required
- Planet name and sign
- Dignity table (domicile, exaltation, detriment, fall)
- Sect of the chart (day/night)

## Dignity Levels

### Strong Dignities
| Level | Description | Score |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Domicile | Planet in its own sign — fully resourced, self-directed | +5 |
| Exaltation | Planet in its sign of honor — heightened, sometimes idealized | +4 |
| Triplicity | Planet in a sign of its element (sect-sensitive) | +3 |
| Bound/Term | Planet in its own degree range | +2 |
| Face/Decan | Planet in its own decan | +1 |

### Debilities
| Level | Description | Score |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Detriment | Planet in sign opposite its domicile — displaced, strained | −5 |
| Fall | Planet in sign opposite its exaltation — humiliated, blocked | −4 |
| Peregrine | Planet in a sign where it has no essential dignity | −1 |

## Interpretation Rules
1. Dignified planets can act — they have resources. Interpret their themes as more accessible.
2. Debilitated planets are stressed — they want to act but face resistance. Themes in that domain require more effort.
3. Peregrine planets are wandering — capable but without a home base. Context-dependent.
4. A dignified malefic (e.g., Mars in Aries) is powerful but still brings Martian themes — just more directed than destructive.
5. A debilitated benefic (e.g., Venus in Aries) loses some of its benevolence — the good it normally brings is harder to access.
6. The score is a guide for the LLM — do not report it numerically to the user.

## Guardrails
- Dignity is not the only factor. A debilitated planet in an angular house may have more impact than a dignified one in a cadent house.
- Do not frame debility as "bad" — frame it as a domain that requires more effort or has specific obstacles.
- Do not use modern rulerships (Uranus/Aquarius, Neptune/Pisces, Pluto/Scorpio) for dignity calculations in traditional mode.
